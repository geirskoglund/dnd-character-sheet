D&D Character Companion uses an adapted, limited subset of the 2014 rules: species walking speeds, selected trait summaries, armor/shield statistics, Unarmored Defense, Fast Movement and Unarmored Movement. Descriptions are condensed and presented with Norwegian UI explanations. This is not the complete SRD library.

Primary source: SRD 5.1 (2014 rules), https://media.wizards.com/2023/downloads/dnd/SRD_CC_v5.1.pdf
