D&D Character Companion uses an adapted subset of the 2014 rules: species walking speeds, selected trait summaries, class/subclass names, armor/shield statistics, Unarmored Defense, Fast Movement and Unarmored Movement. The character catalog also contains 319 spells/cantrips, 407 class-feature entries, the SRD Grappler feat and 237 standard equipment entries, plus curated Cloak of Protection and Ring of Protection facts. Structured weapon profiles, class/multiclass weapon and armor proficiencies, selected Fighting Styles and Martial Arts are adapted from the same 2014 SRD source. Catalog descriptions are adapted from the SRD 5.1; upcasting, material, prerequisites and equipment properties are retained as text. This is not the complete SRD library.

Dataset adaptation: names/IDs normalized, descriptions and prerequisites combined, spells/equipment projected to this application's editable instance model. No automatic rule interpretation from prose. Reproducible importers: scripts/import-srd.mjs and scripts/import-context.mjs. The context dataset adds the 12 class tables (240 levels), hit-die/casting ability facts, structured feature prerequisites and SRD subclass spell grants from the same pinned 2014 source. Selected spell-slot/multiclass, preparation and rest bookkeeping is adapted from these facts; this is not a complete character builder or interpretation of arbitrary rule text.
